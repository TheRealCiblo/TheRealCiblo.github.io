The basics

Big freedom. Clear boundaries.

Griefing and PvP are part of the experience. Cheating and real-world harm are not. By playing on ciblo.xyz, you agree to the rules below.

01 / Allowed

Play the game hard

Griefing, raiding, stealing, PvP, trap kills, base hunting, and AFK farms are allowed. Lost items and griefed bases are not restored.

02 / No cheats

Keep it vanilla

No hacked clients, x-ray, dupes, exploits, macros, fly hacks, lag machines, or crashers. Report exploits instead of using them.

03 / Mods

Performance is fine

Visual and performance mods are welcome. Freecam, entity radar, and cave or entity minimaps are not.

04 / Real-life safety

Never cross the line

No doxxing, threats, or DDoS. Never share real-life personal information. Players may share in-game coordinates.

05 / Conduct

Respect the server

No harassment, hate speech, spam, advertising, ban evasion, or punishment arguments in public chat.

06 / Staff accountability

Power is audited

Staff cannot scout bases, leak information, take items, or favor friends. Every staff command is logged and audited.

Punishments

Fair, consistent, documented.

Staff issue punishment only after reviewing evidence. Context matters, but our standards stay consistent.

Tickets

Every ticket starts in Discord.

Appeals, reports, and support are handled there — not in game or private messages. Include the right details to help us resolve things quickly.

01

Join Discord

Use the link at the top of this page if you are not already in.

02

Open a ticket

Choose the category that best matches what you need.

03

Add evidence

Share usernames, dates, a clear explanation, and any screenshots or clips.

04

Wait for staff

One ticket per issue. Please do not ping staff for an update.

Open a ticket for

  • Cheating or staff abuse reports with evidence
  • Doxxing, threats, or leaked staff coordinates
  • Ban and mute appeals
  • Bugs that affect gameplay

Don’t open a ticket for

  • Griefed bases, stolen items, or PvP deaths
  • Scammed trades
  • Items, ranks, teleports, or rule questions
  • An appeal already answered without new evidence

Staff applications

Help protect the chaos.

We value judgment and integrity over experience. Applications are reviewed in Discord, then shortlisted candidates are interviewed and trialed.

What we look for

  • An active, respectful server and Discord member
  • Honesty about past bans or cheating history
  • Comfort with every command being audited
  • Willingness to complete a limited-permission trial
  • A secured account with two-factor authentication

Think you’re a fit?

Read the rules first, then submit a thoughtful application in Discord. Vague or dishonest applications are declined.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is griefing allowed?

Yes. This is a semi-anarchy server, so raiding, griefing, stealing, and PvP are part of the game. Staff do not restore lost items or bases.

What counts as cheating?

Hacked clients, x-ray, dupes, exploits, kill aura, macros, fly hacks, and lag machines. Cheating earns a tempban; three tempbans become permanent.

How do I appeal a punishment?

Open a Discord ticket with your username, the punishment and date, and any new evidence. Decisions are final unless new evidence is provided.

Can I share coordinates?

Players can share coordinates. Staff cannot share coordinates or player information; doing so means immediate removal.
