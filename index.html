Rules

Play hard. Play fair.

Griefing and PvP are part of the game. Cheating and abuse are not. By playing on ciblo.xyz you accept these rules.

A

Allowed

Griefing, raiding, stealing, PvP, trap kills, base hunting and AFK farms. Staff do not restore griefed bases, lost items or scammed trades.

B

No cheating

No hacked clients, x-ray (including texture packs and ore finders), dupes, exploits, kill aura, auto-clickers, macros, fly hacks, lag machines or crashers. Report exploits instead of using them. Cheating results in a tempban, and three tempbans result in a permanent ban.

C

Mods

Performance and visual mods are allowed. Freecam, entity radar and cave or entity minimaps are not.

D

Real-life harm

No doxxing, threats or DDoS. Never share anyone's real-life personal information. Sharing in-game coordinates is allowed for players.

E

Conduct

No harassment, hate speech, spam or advertising. No ban evasion or alt accounts used to dodge punishment. Do not argue punishments in public chat.

F

Staff conduct

Staff may not spy, scout bases, share coordinates or player information, take items, accept payment or favors, or punish someone they are in a dispute with. All staff commands are logged and audited.

Punishments

Consistent and proportionate.

Action depends on the offense and is at staff discretion. Evidence is required before any punishment is issued.

OffenseTypical action
Chat violations: spam, advertising, harassmentWarning, then tempmute, then tempban
Cheating: hacks, x-ray, dupes, exploitsTempban
Three tempbansPermanent ban
Doxxing, threats, DDoSPermanent ban
Staff sharing coordinates or player informationImmediate removal
Ban evasionPermanent ban
Staff abuse of powersImmediate removal

Decisions are final unless new evidence is provided through an appeal. Every tempban counts toward your total, and three tempbans result in a permanent ban.

Tickets

All tickets are handled in Discord.

We do not accept appeals, reports or support requests anywhere else. Not on this site, not in game, and not in private messages.

Join the Discord

Use the button below if you have not joined yet.

Open a ticket

Go to the tickets channel and choose the category that fits.

Include everything

Username, date, what happened and evidence. Incomplete tickets may be closed.

Wait for a reply

One ticket per issue. Do not ping or message staff about it.

Ban or mute appeal

  • Your Minecraft username
  • Punishment type and date
  • Staff member, if known
  • Why it should be lifted

Player report

  • Your username and the reported player
  • Date, time and type of violation
  • What happened
  • Screenshots or clips

Staff report

  • Your username and the staff member
  • Command or action involved
  • Date, time and what happened
  • Screenshots or clips

Open a ticket for

  • Cheating reports with evidence
  • Staff abuse reports with evidence
  • Doxxing, threats or staff leaking coordinates
  • Ban or mute appeals
  • Bugs that affect gameplay

Do not open a ticket for

  • Griefed bases, stolen items or PvP deaths
  • Scammed trades
  • Questions answered in the rules
  • Requests for items, ranks or teleports
  • Appeals already answered without new evidence

Need help?

Misuse of tickets can lead to ticket access being removed.

Staff Applications

Moderate ciblo.xyz.

Moderators hold powerful commands, so trust matters more than experience. We vet carefully and audit continuously. Staff applications are handled in our Discord.

Apply

Open a staff application in the Discord and answer every question in detail.

Review

Answers and history are reviewed and verified.

Interview

Shortlisted applicants speak with senior staff.

Trial

Limited permissions with mentor oversight and weekly audits.

Full access

Granted only after a clean trial.

Requirements

  • Active member of the server and Discord
  • Honest history. Past bans or cheating must be disclosed
  • Accept that every staff command is logged and audited
  • Accept a trial period with reduced permissions
  • Secure accounts with two-factor authentication

Ready to apply?

Read the rules first. Applications with vague or dishonest answers are rejected.
